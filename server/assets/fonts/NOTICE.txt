PeaceOne bundled resume PDF fonts (SIL Open Font License 1.1; see OFL.txt).

Noto Sans Regular/Bold (TTF)
- Source: https://github.com/notofonts/latin-greek-cyrillic
- Coverage: Latin, extended Latin, Greek, Cyrillic

Noto Sans SC Regular/Bold (Subset OTF)
Noto Sans JP Regular (Subset OTF)
Noto Sans KR Regular (Subset OTF)
- Source: https://github.com/notofonts/noto-cjk
- Coverage: Simplified Chinese, Japanese kana/kanji subset, Korean hangul subset

These files stay in this directory and are registered at PDF generation time.
The server does not download fonts at runtime.

Remaining unsupported scripts include Arabic, Hebrew, Thai, Devanagari, Tamil
and other unbundled complex scripts. Traditional Chinese region-specific glyphs
may render with Simplified Chinese forms. Shared Han ideographs prefer the
Simplified Chinese subset when that font is loaded.
